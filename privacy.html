Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

"Down to Scale" is an internal tool of the Down to Scale project. It is used only by the project's owner to upload videos to the project's own YouTube channel and Instagram account and to read their statistics.

What data is used

  • Google/YouTube: OAuth access only to upload videos to our own channel and to read its statistics.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the project's server and are never shared.

What is not done

  • No personal data of other users is collected.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account permissions. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
